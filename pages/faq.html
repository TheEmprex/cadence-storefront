Getting started

Do I need all five formulas?

No, and most people should not start with all five. Build is the daily base and it is the sensible first purchase. After that, add whichever half of the month is causing the most trouble — Ease for the fortnight before your period, Flow for the first few days. The Cycle Kit bundles those three because together they cover a full month, not because you need all of them to begin.

Where should I actually start?

Take the quiz. It takes about a minute, needs no email, and it will happily tell you that one formula is enough. If you would rather not, the Cycle Kit is the default answer for someone who menstruates and wants the month covered.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Thirty-day returns, opened boxes included. We would rather refund you than have you keep paying for something that is not doing anything — the whole model depends on people staying because it works.

Are these vegan?

Every formula is vegan. Capsules are vegetable cellulose, and there is no gelatin, dairy or marine collagen anywhere in the range.

Is there anything artificial in them?

No artificial sweeteners, colours or flavours. Sweetness in the drink formulas comes from monk fruit and steviol glycosides, and Rest is partly sweet because glycine itself is.

The cycle

My cycle is irregular. Will this still work?

Yes. Build, Rest and Intimate are daily and have nothing to do with your cycle. Ease and Flow are taken by how you feel rather than by a date — the day numbers on this site describe a pattern, they are not a schedule you have to hit. Start Ease when the familiar signs turn up, start Flow on day one of your period.

What if I don't get a period at all?

Build, Rest and Intimate work exactly the same. Ease and Flow are built specifically around the luteal and menstrual phases, so if those do not apply to you, skip them and build a kit from the others. Nothing here requires you to menstruate to be worth taking.

Why isn't there a product for the follicular phase?

Because it is the part of the month most people report feeling good, and we are not going to invent a product for a phase with no felt problem. Build covers the performance side, and the two good weeks do not need anything else.

What if my period lasts longer than five days?

Take Flow for as long as you need it and add a single box when you run short. Five sticks is an average, not a rule.

The formulas

How fast will I notice anything?

It depends entirely on which formula. Rest is usually felt the first night. Flow is same-day. Ease is judged over one or two full cycles. Intimate is gradual over a month or two. Build does nothing at all for three to four weeks — that is saturation, and if you judge it at day ten you will conclude it does not work.

Will creatine make me bulky or bloated?

It will not make you bulky — creatine builds the capacity to train harder, and size still requires the training and the food to go with it. Some people do notice mild water retention in the first fortnight as muscle cells take up more water. It sits inside the muscle rather than under the skin, and it settles.

Can I take them all together?

Yes — that is what the kits are. On a luteal evening you may be taking magnesium from more than one formula, which lands at a moderate combined amount well within normal intake. If you take prescription medication, run the full ingredient list past your doctor rather than assuming.

Why is there no melatonin in Rest?

Because we wanted a sleep formula you could take on a Tuesday with a real Wednesday ahead of you. That is a statement about our product rather than a criticism of melatonin, which is widely used and widely tolerated. The formula leans on glycine at 3 g instead, which is also why Rest is a powder — that dose will not fit in a capsule.

Why only 24 capsules of Ease?

Two a day across twelve luteal days is twenty-four capsules, which is exactly one cycle. Every box we make is sized to a cycle. A bottle of sixty that runs out on a different date to everything else is precisely the problem the range was built to remove.

Do you use branded or patented ingredients?

Where the branded version is genuinely better, yes. Where it mostly buys a logo — creatine being the clearest case — we buy quality raw material and publish our own third-party assay instead, which costs less and produces a certificate with our own lot number on it.

Safety and interactions

I'm on hormonal birth control.

This shaped the formula. Ease uses saffron rather than chasteberry precisely because chasteberry is the ingredient most commonly flagged for interaction with hormonal contraception. Nothing in the range is formulated to act on your hormones. If you take other prescription medication as well, it is worth running the list past your doctor.

I'm pregnant or nursing.

Do not take these. Several ingredients across the range are not appropriate during pregnancy or nursing, and that is a conversation for your doctor or midwife rather than a product page. If you answer honestly in the quiz, it will recommend nothing at all and say the same thing.

I take antidepressants or other prescription medication.

Speak to whoever prescribed them before starting. That is the honest answer for any supplement, and it is a real answer rather than a legal reflex — interactions are specific to the medication, and we cannot assess yours from here.

Is creatine bad for your kidneys?

It is the most persistent rumour in the category. Decades of research in healthy adults has not found the problem it describes. If you have an existing kidney condition, that changes the question and it belongs with your doctor.

Can I drive after taking Rest?

No. Take it thirty to sixty minutes before bed, not before driving or operating machinery, and not with alcohol.

Is there an age limit?

18+. The formulas are dosed for adults and several of them carry warnings that assume an adult reader.

What about allergens?

No gluten, dairy, soy, egg, shellfish or nuts in any formula. Full ingredient lists are on each product page, and the manufacturing facility's allergen controls are documented in the batch paperwork.

Subscription and delivery

How often does it renew?

Every 30 days, flat. Not on a predicted cycle length — cycles vary too much month to month for that to produce better timing rather than missed boxes. The kit is sized generously enough to absorb the drift.

Can I skip a month?

Yes. Skip, pause, move the date or cancel from your account in a couple of clicks. No phone call, no retention script, and no free trial that quietly converts into a subscription.

Can I change what's in my kit?

You can swap between the Cycle Kit and the Full Ritual, and add or remove individual formulas alongside them. The contents of a kit itself are fixed — the kit price exists because the combination is fixed.

What does shipping cost?

Free over $45, which every kit clears. Below that it is a flat rate. Full detail on the shipping and returns page.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. Supplement import rules differ meaningfully by country and we would rather not ship something that gets held at a border and refunded a month later.

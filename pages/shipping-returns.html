Shipping & returns

The boring page, written properly.

Every number here is specific on purpose. A policy page that says "typically" and "may vary" four times is a policy page designed to be argued with later.

Orders are picked and packed within one to two business days, from a fulfilment centre in the United States. Transit times are counted from dispatch, not from when you clicked. Both kits clear the free-shipping threshold on their own.

We ship within the United States only, including Alaska, Hawaii and APO/FPO addresses — those three run a few days longer. We do not ship internationally yet, because supplement import rules differ meaningfully by country and we would rather not sell you something that gets held at a border and refunded a month later.

You will get a tracking number when the parcel leaves, not when the label is created, which are usually a day apart and the reason tracking so often appears to sit still.

Subscription deliveries

Subscriptions renew every 30 days from the date of your first order, on a flat schedule. Not on a predicted cycle length — cycles run anywhere from 21 to 35 days and shift month to month, so tracking them produces missed boxes rather than better timing. Kits are sized generously enough to absorb the drift.

You are charged when the renewal processes, and we send a reminder three days beforehand so a charge never arrives unannounced. From your account you can:

  • Skip the next delivery, and keep the schedule intact after it
  • Move your renewal date forwards or back
  • Swap between the Cycle Kit and the Full Ritual, or add and remove individual formulas
  • Pause indefinitely
  • Cancel outright

All of it takes a couple of clicks. There is no phone call, no chat queue and no retention script, and there is no free trial that quietly converts into a subscription — that structure is most of why this category has the reputation it has.

Cancel any time before a renewal processes and you are not charged for it. Cancel after, and that box still ships and is still returnable under the policy below.

Returns

Thirty days, and opened boxes count. A supplement you have not tried tells you nothing, so a returns policy that only accepts sealed product is a returns policy designed not to be used.

Email us with your order number within 30 days of delivery and we will refund the purchase price to your original payment method. There is no restocking fee and you do not need to ship anything back — for opened supplements that is both cheaper for us and a nonstarter for resale, so it would only be theatre.

Refunds land in 5–10 business days depending on your bank. Shipping charges are refunded when the return is our fault and not when it is a change of mind, which is the usual arrangement and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

One request rather than a rule: if you are returning something because it did not work, tell us which formula and how long you gave it. Build genuinely does nothing for three to four weeks and Ease takes a cycle or two, and a meaningful share of returns are timing rather than the product.

Damaged, missing or wrong

Send a photo of the parcel and its contents within 14 days of delivery and we will replace it. No return needed.

If tracking says delivered and the parcel is not there, give it 48 hours — carriers scan early more often than they lose things. Still missing after that, tell us and we will send a replacement rather than open a carrier investigation and make it your problem.

Cancelling mid-cycle

Nothing is prorated because nothing is billed in advance of a shipment. You pay for a box when it ships. Cancel and the boxes stop; there is no remaining term, no minimum number of deliveries and nothing to buy out.

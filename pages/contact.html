One thing we can't help with

We cannot tell you whether a formula is safe alongside your prescription, whether a symptom you are having needs looking at, or what is causing it. Not because of a legal reflex — because we genuinely do not know, and a supplement company guessing at that is how people get hurt.

What we can do is send you the complete ingredient list with doses so your doctor or pharmacist has something concrete to work from. Ask and we will send it the same day.

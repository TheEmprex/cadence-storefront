The quiz

Six questions. Then we'll tell you exactly what to take.

No email required to see your result, and no obligation to buy the answer. If the honest recommendation is one formula rather than five, that's what you'll get.

Prefer to work it out yourself? The how it works page lays out the same logic without the questions, and the shop lists everything with filters.

About the recommendation

How the answer gets built.

Why does everyone get Build?

Because it's the base, and it's the only formula in the line that doesn't move with the month. Creatine works by saturation rather than by dose-on-the-day, so it's taken every day regardless of phase. If you'd rather not take it, buy the other formulas individually — nothing forces the kit.

Will it always try to sell me the biggest kit?

No. The recommendation is built from what you actually answered. Say sleep is fine and you won't be shown Rest. Say the second half of your month is uneventful and Ease drops out. If you select pregnant or nursing, the quiz recommends nothing at all and points you to your doctor.

Do you store my answers?

Your result is kept in your own browser so the site can remember it if you come back. It isn't sent anywhere, and there's no email step in front of the result.

What if my cycle is irregular or I don't get one?

Answer honestly and the result adjusts. Build, Rest and Intimate work with no cycle involved at all, and Ease and Flow are taken by how you feel rather than on a date.

Questions

The ones that actually get asked.

Do I need all five?

No. Build is the daily base and most people start there, adding Ease or Flow depending on which part of the cycle is giving them the most trouble. The Cycle Kit exists because those three together are what a full month looks like — but starting with one formula is a perfectly reasonable way in.

I'm on hormonal birth control. Does that change anything?

It changed the formula. We left chasteberry out of Ease specifically because it is the ingredient most commonly flagged for interaction with hormonal contraception, and used saffron instead. Nothing in the line is formulated to act on your hormones. If you take other prescription medication, still worth a word with your doctor.

My cycle is irregular, or I don't get a period.

Build, Rest and Intimate all stand on their own with no cycle involved. Ease and Flow are taken by how you feel rather than by a date — the day numbers on this site are a shorthand, not a schedule you have to hit.

How fast will I notice anything?

It depends on the formula and we would rather say so up front. Rest tends to be felt the first night. Flow is same-day. Ease is judged over one or two full cycles. Build takes three to four weeks to reach saturation — genuinely nothing before then.
